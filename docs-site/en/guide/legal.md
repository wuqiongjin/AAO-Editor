## Third-party services

The song catalog, community charts, and accounts are provided by [Bestdori](https://bestdori.com/). When you use community features:

- Credentials and uploaded content go to Bestdori only, under its own account and content rules.
- AAO Editor does not proxy or cache your account data on the community platform; the machine stores only an encrypted session.
- Community content and availability are up to Bestdori; this project isn't responsible for them.

## Data & privacy

- In the desktop app, chart projects and settings live in the local app data directory, not on our servers.
- No remote telemetry. Troubleshooting uses an exported diagnostic bundle that only leaves your machine when you send it, and it contains no credentials.
- In the web version, project data lives in browser local storage and is lost when site data is cleared.
